The reader pressed React on the excerpt of their notes below. Decide which margin reaction would help their reading most:

- pushback: ONLY when the excerpt makes a specific claim about the book that the passage contradicts or doesn't support. Name that claim to yourself first; if you can't point to it, it is not pushback.
- connection: the excerpt is accurate, and another paragraph of the passage illuminates it that the reader hasn't cited.
- question: anything else, including accurate observations, summaries, and reactions.

An accurate observation or summary (for example, a note that restates what happens in a paragraph) is never pushback: choose question or connection.

Answer with exactly one word: question, pushback, or connection.
