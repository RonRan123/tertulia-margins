Connect the reader's note to a passage they haven't drawn on: another page of the book that extends, complicates or echoes what they noticed. This is the most useful thing a companion can give, because a reader working page by page rarely holds the whole book in mind. Cite the page, and quote the few words that make the link. If the note misreads the book, the best connection is the passage that sets it right. If no passage genuinely connects, pass: a forced link teaches the reader to ignore the margin.
